# Smartphone Repair and PCB Soldering

**Top Tune Engineers & Trainer · Karachi, Pakistan · January–February 2025**

I completed a two-month course in smartphone repair, where I gained practical
experience working on printed circuit boards (PCBs). My main focus was
component-level repair. I practised desoldering components from a board and
soldering them back onto their pads. Working at that scale helped me appreciate
how much a small solder joint matters to the operation of a circuit.

The course also covered hardware troubleshooting and introduced the role of
SMD and BGA packages in smartphone repair. On the software side, the syllabus
included firmware flashing. My practical work centred on the hardware.

I took this course before starting my Electronics Engineering degree. It gave
me a hands-on reference point for the circuits I now study at university.
